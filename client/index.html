Formula: monthly items × (manual minutes − assisted minutes, including review) × assumed case share ÷ 60. Values are monthly run-rates at each scenario point—not cumulative totals.

Capacity value is not cash saved. The model excludes build and operating cost, exceptions, case mix, rollout effort, and other factors. Real impact needs an agreed baseline and a measured pilot.

0–3 MONTHS · MEASURE

Baseline → controlled pilot

Agree the starting touch time, quality, exception rate, rework, and human sign-off. Compare the pilot to that baseline; timing depends on scope.

3–6 MONTHS · EARN THE NEXT STEP

Evidence → refine or expand

Keep, change, or extend a workflow only when quality and control hold. Measure where released capacity goes; do not assume it becomes a cash saving.

06 / BUILT WITH INTENTION

Useful automation.
Clear accountability.

AI can help move information. It shouldn’t obscure who is responsible for the next decision.

Our working principles ↓
01

Fit the process—not the trend.

Start with the work and its constraints. Use AI only where it adds a useful capability.

02

Keep people in the loop.

Important decisions, approvals, and exceptions stay visible and accountable to people.

03

Meet the tools already in use.

Plan around your operating environment. Feasibility and integration scope are validated before build.

04

Make it work across languages.

Design for the languages your team and customers use, then confirm coverage for the specific workflow.
